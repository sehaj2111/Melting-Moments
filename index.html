            Simple Answers
          
          

            Frequently Asked Questions
          

        


        

          

            

              How far in advance should I message you to place an order?
            

            

              For our signature classic flavours (Belgian Chocolate Truffle, Ferrero Rocher, Red Velvet, Fresh Fruit, or Butterscotch), we offer same-day delivery in 2 to 3 hours. For custom designer cakes, fondant themes, and multi-tier Roka or wedding cakes, please message us 2 to 4 days ahead.
            

          


          

            

              Are your cakes 100% eggless?
            

            

              Yes! We specialise in 100% eggless artisanal sponges and frostings baked fresh from scratch so every member of your family can celebrate together.
            

          


          

            

              Can I send a reference picture from Instagram or Pinterest?
            

            

              Of course. Use the Order Builder above to pick your weight and date, then send us your reference photo directly on WhatsApp or Instagram DM (@melting_moments01) and Vanshika will personalise the colours and flavours for you.
            

          


          

            

              How do you safely deliver tiered cakes across Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula?
            

            

              Every cake is chilled to set its structure, packed in a sturdy tall celebration box, and delivered with care across Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula, or prepared for studio pickup in Dashmesh Enclave.
